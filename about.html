Why is education so important?

Well-educated people play essential roles in shaping the world through their contributions in various fields, promoting progress and development and influencing the positive changes across the diverse communities such as: economic growth, innovative and technological advancements, social justice, cultural enrichment & many more. Therefore, education must be accessible for everyone.

What is the issue related to education that we want to raise awareness about?

The answer is the lack of access to education around the world.

Despite the fact that education is one of the vital parts of people's lives, there are still many people in the world who don’t have access to education, which limits their opportunities for personal growth and the ability to contribute meaningfully to their communities and society as a whole, which is pretty unfair.

There are numerous reasons why this issue exists, some of which are financial barriers, infrastructure and distances in rural areas, gender inequality, lack of accommodations for the people who are disabled or have special needs, lack of awareness and etc.

This is why we want to encourage you to:

➯ Raise awareness about this issue in your own community.

➯ Share about this issue on your social media and encourage more people to join this cause and raise awareness.

➯ Volunteer! If you have some free time during your day, you can always do tutoring or mentoring sessions for students.

➯ Donate if you can! You can always contribute financially to support educational initiatives, which provide learning spaces, or scholarships and financial aid to those in need.
