EmpowerED: A Global Impact Initiative to Break Barriers for Education Access

By understanding the importance of education and the issues with its accessibility, our mission is to encourage people to advocate, support and take actions to break down all the barriers, empower communities and make sure that everyone has the opportunity to receive high-quality education to reach their full potential.

In our opinion:

➯ Acquiring education is a basic human right.

➯ Everyone should have equal opportunities for gaining knowledge.

➯ Equitable education should be provided for all, regardless of their cultural, racial, religious or any other type of background.

➯ Education serves as a pathway to success therefore it should be provided to everyone.
